## Current strongest interpretation

Defensible wording:

> In this Seattle/frame-0 wrist-z diagnostic, the pretrained HUGS learned deformation field introduces small cross-joint LBS components that are strongly associated with the amplified contralateral Gaussian response relative to the SMPL-derived K=6 target and SMPL-only control.

Do not yet say the learned weights have been proven causal. Step 13E is very strong mechanism-level evidence and association, but the causal counterfactual has not been run.

## Exact next step for the next chat

Start with **Step 14 - counterfactual LBS ablation**.

Keep all other quantities fixed and compare the same left-wrist +10 deg perturbation under:

1. original learned LBS,
2. reconstructed K=6 target LBS,
3. learned LBS with only left-wrist + left-hand channels zeroed for the contralateral subset and renormalized.

Primary question:

> Does the contralateral displacement collapse when the learned cross-joint wrist component is removed?

If yes, this provides direct causal evidence for the learned cross-joint skinning mechanism. If no, inspect transform construction and coordinate/lever-arm effects next.

Do not start another broad descriptive sweep before this ablation.
