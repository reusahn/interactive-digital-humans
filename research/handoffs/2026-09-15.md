## Current status

Current research question:

> Do small learned cross-joint LBS components in HUGS causally mediate the stable contralateral upper-body response observed under a local left-wrist perturbation, and does that mechanism survive changes in base pose and independent pretrained sequences?

For the pretrained NeuMan Seattle model, the answer is now **yes under the fixed counterfactual tested today**, and the effect remains highly stable across four tested evaluation poses.
