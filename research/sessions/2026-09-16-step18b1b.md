# Research Session Checkpoint - 2026-09-16 Step 18B1B

## Purpose

Test whether the Step-18B1 elbow regression blocker came only from displacement evaluation order, specifically direct `delta-A` displacement versus computing full float32 before/after positions first.

No shoulder causal computation was performed. No threshold, joint, mask, branch, pose, axis, or perturbation was changed.

## Result

The evaluation-order hypothesis was only partially supported.

Computing full before/after positions substantially reduced the learned-condition discrepancy relative to the frozen Step-17B1 arrays, but did **not** restore the frozen `1e-5` contralateral-sum regression gate for the learned condition in any of the three checkpoints.

### Learned condition

| Checkpoint | delta-A contra error | best full-position contra error | frozen gate |
|---|---:|---:|---|
| Seattle | `-3.667395e-4` | `-2.927389e-5` | FAIL |
| Parkinglot | `-1.622576e-3` | `+4.781559e-5` | FAIL |
| Jogging | `-7.595423e-5` | `+1.115697e-5` | FAIL |

All remain spatially almost identical to the archived fields, with Pearson values effectively 1 and mean per-Gaussian absolute differences around `5e-8` to `1e-7`.

### K6 condition

All K6 variants pass the `1e-5` contralateral-sum regression tolerance across all three checkpoints.

For Jogging K6, the full-position path reduces contralateral error to about `4.48e-8`.

### Cross-checkpoint synthesis

Each tested evaluation-order variant produced `3/6` condition-level contralateral regression passes because K6 passes and learned fails in all three checkpoints.

The best learned full-position residuals are much smaller than the direct delta-A residuals but remain outside the frozen tolerance.

### Seattle historical position check

The reconstructed Seattle elbow positions differ from the historical baseline probe only at tiny float32 scale:

- before mean abs difference: `5.063156e-08`
- before max abs difference: `9.536743e-07`
- after mean abs difference: `4.979415e-08`
- after max abs difference: `9.536743e-07`

Neither before nor after is bitwise exact.

## Interpretation

Evaluation order is part of the discrepancy but is not the whole explanation. The remaining mismatch likely lies earlier in the exact SMPL/HUGS transform computation or in backend/hardware floating-point execution order.

Because the historical Step-17 arrays are internally stable while the current CPU reconstruction differs only at distributed float32 scale, the next audit should compare the manual transform path directly against the exact `smplx` functions used by HUGS and, if necessary, compare one-shot versus chunked matrix multiplication.

Do not widen the frozen regression tolerance.
