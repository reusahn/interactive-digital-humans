## Strongest bounded interpretation

Across three independently pretrained HUGS NeuMan checkpoints, the same learned cross-joint LBS causal mechanism is observed for two tested joints, left wrist and the predeclared left elbow. Replacing learned deformation weights with the subject-specific SMPL-derived K6 target removes at least 99.708575% of the tested contralateral response, while selective ablation of the corresponding learned branch components eliminates the tested contralateral response under all 36 nested pose diagnostics. Removed branch mass remains strongly correlated with displacement reduction, with global minimum correlation 0.968898.

This is evidence for cross-joint and within-checkpoint pose robustness under the tested protocol. It is not evidence from six independent models and should not be presented as 36 independent replications.
