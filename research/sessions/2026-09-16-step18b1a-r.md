## Interpretation

The mismatch is not evidence of a different deformation field. Pearson agreement is effectively 1 and per-Gaussian absolute differences are approximately 1e-8 to 1e-7 on average. The aggregate tolerance still fails because these tiny differences accumulate over hundreds of thousands of Gaussians.

A leading untested hypothesis is floating-point evaluation order: Step-18B1 currently computes displacement directly from `delta_A = A_after - A_before`, whereas the earlier Step-17 pipeline may have generated full float32 before/after posed positions and then taken their difference/norm. These paths are algebraically equivalent but need not be bitwise equivalent in float32.

This hypothesis must be tested directly. Do not widen tolerance and do not compute shoulder metrics before the historical elbow regression is repaired.
