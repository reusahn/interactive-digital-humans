## Steps 14C-14D - counterfactual setup and mask correction

Three conditions were defined:

1. original learned HUGS LBS,
2. reconstructed SMPL-derived K=6 target LBS,
3. learned LBS with only left-wrist + left-hand channels removed on the high-confidence contralateral subset, then row-renormalized.

The first pass used 32,603 contralateral Gaussians. This disagreed with Step 13E's 33,072 count. The discrepancy was fully explained by omission of `right_collar`.

```text
right_collar high-confidence count: 469
32603 + 469 = 33072
```

The same audit established that `left_collar` should be included in the same-side local chain.

Corrected anatomical definitions:

```text
intended:
left_wrist + left_hand

same-side local chain:
left_collar + left_shoulder + left_elbow

contralateral upper body:
right_collar + right_shoulder + right_elbow + right_wrist + right_hand
```
