## Interpretation

Parkinglot now shows a pose-stable causal mechanism, not a single-frame accident. Across all four tested evaluation poses, learned contralateral displacement remains tightly normalized at about 3.39% of high-confidence displacement. Replacing learned LBS with the corresponding SMPL-derived K=6 target removes about 99.71% of the contralateral response at every frame. Selectively removing only learned left-wrist/left-hand channels on the fixed contralateral subset removes the contralateral response completely at every frame.

The `fraction displacement reduced` values of approximately 0.84 to 0.86 do not conflict with zero ablated contralateral sum. That metric uses the strict condition `delta > 0`; contralateral Gaussians that already had exactly zero learned displacement are not counted as reduced. The aggregate ablated contralateral displacement is exactly zero in all four frames.

## Strongest current claim

The same learned cross-joint wrist/hand LBS mechanism has now been observed causally and shown to be pose-stable in **two independently pretrained HUGS NeuMan checkpoints**, Seattle and Parkinglot, across four tested evaluation poses in each checkpoint.

This still does not establish a universal HUGS failure. Evidence is currently concentrated on one joint (`left_wrist`), one perturbation axis (`z`), and one perturbation family.
