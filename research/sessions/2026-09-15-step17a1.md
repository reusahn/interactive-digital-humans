
## Result

The discrepancy is **not** caused by `>= 0.9` versus `> 0.9` threshold semantics. There are no exact confidence values at `0.9` in either source.

Four source/comparator variants were tested:

```text
recomputed >= : HC 197781, contra 33074
recomputed >  : HC 197781, contra 33074
saved >=      : HC 197778, contra 33072
saved >       : HC 197778, contra 33072
```

Only the saved anatomy fields in the Seattle Step-13A artifact reproduce the already-frozen corrected benchmark counts.

The relevant Step-13A NPZ fields are:

- `saved_dominant`
- `saved_confidence`

Compared with `argmax/max(effective_lbs)`:

```text
dominant-joint mismatch count: 2
confidence mean absolute error: 1.821307887439616e-05
confidence max absolute error:  0.1323409080505371
HC decision differences:        5 rows
contralateral differences:      2 rows
```

HC decision-difference indices:

```text
[239232, 269473, 287191, 296137, 299167]
```

Contralateral decision-difference indices:

```text
[239232, 269473]
```

No row lies within `5e-6` of confidence `0.9`, ruling out a floating-point threshold-boundary explanation.

Experiment-05 metadata independently confirms that the corrected Seattle causal benchmark used `num_high_confidence = 197778` and `num_contralateral = 33072`.

## Interpretation

Seattle has a historical anatomy-source distinction inside the Step-13A artifact. The saved anatomy fields are the source that reproduces the previously reviewed and frozen Seattle benchmark. Recomputing anatomy from `effective_lbs` changes five HC decisions and two contralateral memberships.

This does **not** alter the frozen second-joint protocol. It is an implementation/provenance reconciliation discovered before any elbow perturbation. For Seattle second-joint work, the anatomical mask must remain consistent with the frozen Seattle benchmark and therefore use `saved_dominant` / `saved_confidence` from Step 13A while the actual K6 deformation condition continues to use `effective_lbs`.
