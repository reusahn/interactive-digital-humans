## Interpretation guardrails

- Independent model-level `n = 3` checkpoints.
- The 18 frame tests are nested within those checkpoints and are within-checkpoint pose-robustness diagnostics, not 18 independent replications.
- Raw displacement sums should not be pooled across sequences because subject/sequence scale differs.
- Preferred cross-checkpoint comparison quantities are normalized contralateral `% HC`, reduction ratios, and removed-mass/displacement correlations.
- The frozen finding is bounded to HUGS NeuMan checkpoints and the tested left-wrist perturbation. It is not yet a result about other joints or Gaussian-human methods generally.
