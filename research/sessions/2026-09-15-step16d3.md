## Interpretation

Jogging is **not yet a third causal replication** because no pose perturbation or intervention has been applied. However, the pre-perturbation mechanism precursor is now present in a third independently pretrained checkpoint.

The next decisive test is the same frozen-mask causal diagnostic used for the first two models: Jogging raw frame 2, `left_wrist z +10 deg`, comparing original learned LBS, subject-specific K=6 target, and learned LBS with only left-wrist/left-hand channels removed on the fixed 20,887-Gaussian contralateral subset.
