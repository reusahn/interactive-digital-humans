## Decision

```text
THIRD CAUSAL REPLICATION CANDIDATE: True
```

This is the first point at which the same bounded causal mechanism has been reproduced in **three independently pretrained HUGS NeuMan checkpoints**: Seattle, Parkinglot, and Jogging.

The evidence is still bounded to the tested mechanism family, especially `left_wrist` perturbations and SMPL-derived K6 anatomical baselines. This is not evidence that all joints or all HUGS failures behave identically.

## Strongest defensible claim after Step 16D4

> In three independently pretrained HUGS NeuMan checkpoints, Seattle, Parkinglot, and Jogging, small learned cross-joint left-wrist/hand LBS components causally mediate an amplified contralateral upper-body displacement relative to the corresponding subject-specific SMPL-derived K=6 target under the tested left-wrist perturbation. Selectively removing only those learned wrist/hand channels on the fixed contralateral subset eliminates the tested contralateral response.

Seattle and Parkinglot also have within-checkpoint pose replication. Parkinglot additionally has x/y/z and sign robustness. Jogging has not yet undergone pose replication.
