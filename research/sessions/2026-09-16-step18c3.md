## Scientific interpretation

Within this exploratory shoulder decomposition, **per-row shoulder-descendant support mass and support topology dominate the learned-vs-K6 displacement-field difference**.

The result is stronger than an aggregate-mass statement. K6 has zero shoulder-descendant branch mass on most contralateral rows, whereas learned HUGS has positive branch mass on all tested contralateral rows. Step 18C2 explicitly matching each row's K6 branch mass, including setting the branch mass to zero where K6 has zero support, removes most of the spatial field gap. Step 18C3 preserving learned branch mass while changing only within-branch allocation produces essentially no field-gap reduction and sometimes worsens the match.

Therefore the current best exploratory mechanism statement is:

> For the tested shoulder perturbation, learned-vs-K6 cross-body differences are driven primarily by the **presence and magnitude of per-Gaussian shoulder-descendant support**, rather than by the distribution of a fixed amount of branch mass among joints 16/18/20/22.
