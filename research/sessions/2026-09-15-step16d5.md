## Interpretation

Jogging now supplies both a third independent checkpoint-level causal replication and strong within-checkpoint pose robustness across all 10 effective evaluation poses. The contralateral fraction of high-confidence displacement remains tightly bounded around `8.34-8.67%`, K6 replacement removes at least `99.81779%` of the tested contralateral response at every frame, and the selective wrist/hand intervention removes `100%` at every frame.

These 10 frame tests are not 10 independent model replications. They are within-checkpoint pose robustness tests sharing one checkpoint, learned deformation field, Gaussian set, K6 target, and fixed anatomical mask.

## Strongest bounded result after Step 16D5

The same left-wrist/hand cross-joint causal mechanism has now been reproduced in three independently pretrained HUGS NeuMan checkpoints: Seattle, Parkinglot, and Jogging. Seattle and Parkinglot are pose-stable across four tested evaluation poses, and Jogging is pose-stable across all 10 effective evaluation poses. Parkinglot additionally passes the tested x/y/z and perturbation-sign robustness control.

Do not generalize this yet to all HUGS checkpoints, other joints, or other Gaussian-human methods.
