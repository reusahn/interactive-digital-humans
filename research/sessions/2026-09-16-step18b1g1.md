## Interpretation

Moving from CPU to CUDA does not by itself recover the frozen Step-17B1 arrays. Strict CUDA FP32 improves Seattle and Jogging enough to pass the aggregate gate, but Parkinglot learned remains outside the frozen tolerance. The remaining discrepancy is not a scientific-effect mismatch: field correlations are effectively 1 and errors are distributed at float32 scale. It is a historical runtime numerical-equivalence problem.

Because prior Step-17B1 output explicitly records `deformation device: cuda`, the leading unresolved factor is now historical CUDA environment provenance, especially PyTorch/CUDA/cuBLAS execution details and exact norm/position evaluation order, not CPU-versus-CUDA selection itself.

No shoulder result is accepted and no tolerance is widened.
