The anatomical subset was fixed before displacement using the Parkinglot-specific SMPL-derived K=6 target reconstructed in Step 16C2.

## Interpretation

This is a successful causal replication of the Seattle mechanism in a second independently pretrained HUGS checkpoint.

The strongest defensible claim after Step 16C3 is:

> In two independently pretrained HUGS NeuMan checkpoints, Seattle and Parkinglot, small learned cross-joint left-wrist/hand LBS components causally mediate an amplified contralateral upper-body displacement under a left-wrist z perturbation relative to the corresponding SMPL-derived K=6 target.

This is stronger than a same-checkpoint pose replication, but it is still bounded to two checkpoints and the tested left-wrist z perturbation. It should not yet be generalized to all joints, axes, NeuMan sequences, or Gaussian-human methods.

The exact zero under selective ablation is structurally expected under pure LBS because the perturbation changes only left-wrist and descendant left-hand transforms. The novel evidence is the full chain: K=6 target has almost no contralateral wrist/hand support, learned HUGS introduces such support, learned HUGS exhibits the amplified cross-body response, K=6 replacement nearly removes it, and selective removal of the learned causal channels eliminates it.
