# 2026-09-15 - Counterfactual LBS Ablation, Seattle Frame Replication, and Cross-Sequence Setup

## Research question

Does the stable contralateral upper-body response under a controlled left-wrist perturbation collapse when the learned cross-joint left-wrist/hand LBS components are removed while canonical Gaussians, pose transforms, geometry, and all other learned weights are held fixed, does that mechanism remain stable across multiple base poses from the same pretrained Seattle model, and can it be reproduced in an independently pretrained sequence?

## Step 14B - independent forward reconstruction

Before running any counterfactual, the HUGS learned-LBS deformation path was reconstructed independently from the saved canonical Gaussians, SMPL pose parameters, learned LBS weights, and the HUGS Vitruvian-to-pose transform convention.

A split-index correction was discovered during validation. `probe frame_index=0` corresponds to raw NeuMan frame **2**, not raw frame 22. The HUGS dataset code stores the third return value of `get_data_splits()` as `val_split`, so the relevant raw-frame sequence is the test-list portion `[2, 7, 12, 17]`.

At raw frame 2, reconstruction against the saved HUGS probe was essentially exact:

```text
before mean xyz error:       2.363707e-7
after mean xyz error:        2.363728e-7
displacement mean abs error: 7.917442e-8
displacement p99 abs error:  4.468192e-7
displacement correlation:    0.9999999992187057
saved total displacement:    214.3095355
reconstructed total:         214.3024663
```

This validates the independent LBS forward path used for the counterfactual.

## Steps 14C-14D - mask reconciliation

The first counterfactual pass produced 32,603 high-confidence contralateral Gaussians, whereas Step 13E had reported 33,072. The difference was traced exactly to omission of the right-collar joint from the Step 14C mask:

```text
right-collar high-confidence count: 469
32603 + 469 = 33072
```

The same audit showed that the same-side local-chain definition should include the left collar. Step 14E therefore restored the exact Step 13E upper-body definitions rather than silently changing the mask.

## Step 14E - corrected causal counterfactual

Three conditions were compared under the same left-wrist z +10 degree perturbation:

1. original learned HUGS LBS,
2. reconstructed SMPL-derived K=6 target LBS,
3. learned LBS with only the left-wrist and left-hand channels zeroed on the exact high-confidence contralateral subset, followed by row renormalization.

No weights outside the 33,072-Gaussian contralateral subset were changed.

## Interpretation

The Step 14 counterfactual provides direct causal evidence **within the fixed Seattle diagnostic** that the learned cross-joint left-wrist/hand LBS component is necessary for the observed high-confidence contralateral upper-body displacement.

Step 15A strengthens this by showing that the same mechanism is highly stable across four different Seattle evaluation base poses. However, these four tests are not independent model replications because they share the same pretrained checkpoint, learned weights, canonical Gaussian set, and anatomical mask.

The strongest current claim is:

> In the pretrained HUGS Seattle sequence, learned cross-joint wrist/hand LBS components causally mediate an amplified contralateral upper-body response under left-wrist z perturbation, and this mechanism remains stable across four tested evaluation poses.

Do **not** generalize this yet to other NeuMan sequences, independently trained HUGS models, joints, axes, or Gaussian-human methods.
