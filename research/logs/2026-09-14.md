## Metric-definition clarification

Two locality scales had initially been mixed together for the ankle probe.

The strict joint-subtree diagnostic treats:

```text
left_ankle -> left_ankle + left_foot
```

while the coarse semantic region used by the probe helper defines:

```text
left_leg -> left_hip + left_knee + left_ankle + left_foot
```

Thus the baseline ankle-z value of **26.57%** measures fine ankle/foot leakage and counts propagation into left knee/hip as outside. The coarse full-left-leg control answers a different question: how much motion leaves the entire ipsilateral leg.

This distinction replaces the earlier binary valid/invalid interpretation.

## Current interpretation

The strongest defensible statement today is:

> Under this Seattle/frame-0 wrist-z diagnostic, the HUGS Gaussian deformation pipeline exhibits a stable amplification of normalized nonlocal and especially contralateral-arm displacement relative to an SMPL-only vertex control.

This does **not** yet establish a HUGS representation failure. The comparison still differs in spatial support:

- SMPL has 6,890 vertices,
- HUGS has approximately 473k Gaussians,
- Gaussian density is nonuniform,
- HUGS uses a K=6 Gaussian-to-SMPL blended transform,
- dominant-joint labels are only a diagnostic grouping.

Absolute displacement sums and element counts should therefore not be compared directly across representations.

---

## Next question

Does the amplification arise from HUGS's Gaussian-to-SMPL support mapping itself?

The next low-cost experiment should reconstruct HUGS's **actual K=6 effective LBS weights** for the saved Gaussian canonical coordinates. For every Gaussian, use the official HUGS K=6 weighting rule and test whether the Gaussians contributing to the contralateral residual receive unexpected left-wrist / left-arm influence through their nearby Vitruvian SMPL vertices.

If the mapping predicts the observed cross-body subset, the mechanism is likely support interpolation / skinning assignment. If the mapping does not explain it, the next stage is to instrument full HUGS transforms directly.
